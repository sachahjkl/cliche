**New workflow steps: Shorten URL, Open, Delete file, Ask, Pin to the screen, Make QR code**: an address is shortened with is.gd, v.gd or a ShareX URL shortener; an address opens in the browser and a file with its program; a file is deleted, for example once Upload's new "done" fires; Ask shows a question in a notification and its "yes" and "no" decide what runs; a picture is pinned over the other windows; a text becomes a QR code's picture.
