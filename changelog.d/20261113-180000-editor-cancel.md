**The editor has a Cancel button, and a workflow can wait for Done or Cancel.** An Annotate step has a "done" and a "cancelled" output: link one to the "when" of a step that copies, saves, uploads, notifies or keeps the picture in the history, and that step runs only when you press Done, or only when you cancel. Cancel skips what needs the edited picture, as closing the editor did.
