**Turn, mirror, resize, add margins, cut out, trim**: the editor's Picture menu turns the picture (Ctrl+R, Ctrl+Shift+R), mirrors it, resizes it (with its ratio kept or not), adds margins of a colour, and trims its plain edges (Ctrl+Shift+X); the Cut out tool (K) removes a band of rows or columns and joins the rest; the crop has handles to adjust it after it is drawn. The annotations follow the picture, and each change is undone in one step.
