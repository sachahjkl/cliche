**The settings have the pages every program of the toolkit has.** Window, Scrolling, Input, Delays and menus, and Rendering join cliche's own pages, with the text and fonts under Appearance; each setting applies at once in every window. Technical ones wait under a folded Advanced part. Themes of your own go in a file the Appearance page links to, and the settings export to JSON, TOML or INI and import back.
